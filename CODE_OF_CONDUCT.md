# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Zryna community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when mistakes happen, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[conduct@zryna.com](mailto:conduct@zryna.com). Project maintainers will review reports fairly,
protect the reporter's privacy where possible, and take appropriate corrective action. Use the
separate process in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) only for software vulnerabilities.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
